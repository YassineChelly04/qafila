# 02 — Brand system: Qafila

## Personality & voice

- **Grounded, precise, warm.** Heritage of the caravan routes + the precision of modern tracking.
- French, vouvoiement, short sentences, concrete numbers ("Livré en 24 h dans le Grand Tunis"), no hype words ("révolutionnaire", "solution innovante", "synergie" are banned).
- Arabic is used as an accent, never as decoration-for-decoration: the wordmark قافلة, section kickers like «الطريق» (la route), status words.

## Typography — strict

| Role | Font | Source / licence |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines (Latin) | **Funnel Display** (300–800) | Google Fonts, OFL — self-hosted woff2 in `landing/assets/fonts/` |
| Body & UI (Latin) | **Familjen Grotesk** (400–700) | Google Fonts, OFL — self-hosted |
| **All Arabic text** | **Thmanyah** (Sans for UI/labels, Serif Display for big Arabic headings) | font.thmanyah.com — personal/school use. Files live in `landing/assets/fonts/thmanyah/` and are **never** committed/redistributed (see 05-AGENT-RULES) |

**Banned fonts:** Inter, Poppins, Roboto, Open Sans, Montserrat, Lato, IBM Plex (any), Space Grotesk, Space Mono, JetBrains Mono, any monospace "techy" font, Reem Kufi/Cairo/Tajawal for Arabic (Thmanyah only).
Numbers (prices, tracking refs, KPIs): use Familjen Grotesk with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` — not a monospace font.

Fallback stacks:
```css
--font-display: "Funnel Display", "Familjen Grotesk", system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-body: "Familjen Grotesk", system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-arabic: "Thmanyah Sans", "Noto Sans Arabic", sans-serif;
--font-arabic-display: "Thmanyah Serif Display", "Thmanyah Sans", serif;
```

| `--blue` | Bleu Sidi Bou Saïd | `#1F5FAD` | the village's doors & grilles | primary brand colour, links, Odoo primary |
| `--brick` | Brique de Tozeur | `#C27C2C` | ochre bricks of Ouled El Hadef | route lines, highlights, illustrations |
| `--henna` | Henné | `#B4472A` | terracotta / henna | CTAs, alerts, important accents |

Contrast checks (WCAG): Henné on Chaux ≈ 7:1; white on Bleu ≈ 6.4:1; Ink on Sand > 11:1. Never put Brick text on Sand (low contrast) — Brick is for strokes/shapes only.
Ratio guideline: ~60 % Chaux/Sand, 25 % Ink, 10 % Blue, 5 % Henna/Brick. Never a purple/indigo gradient, never neon.

## Logo

- **Wordmark:** Latin "qafila" (Funnel Display, lowercase, tight tracking) + Arabic «قافلة» (Thmanyah).
- **Symbol:** a route line with three stops (caravan stages) ending in a filled stop = "arrived". Files in `brand/logo/` (SVG: full colour, white, ink, symbol-only; PNG exports for Odoo).
- Clear space = height of the "q". Minimum symbol size 24 px.
- Don't: stretch, recolour outside the palette, add shadows/gradients, put on busy photos without an Ink overlay.

## Visual motifs

- Route lines with stops (dashed Brick lines, round nodes).
- Geometric patterns from Tunisian zellige / Tozeur brickwork, used at low opacity as textures — never as busy wallpaper.
- Map of Tunisia with the main corridors (Tunis–Sousse–Sfax–Gabès, Tunis–Kairouan–Gafsa–Tozeur, Tunis–Bizerte, Tunis–Nabeul).
- Illustrations are flat, 2–3 palette colours, generous negative space. No stock-photo clichés.

## Motion

One orchestrated system: the scroll-driven **voyage** (below). The illustration itself is static — no SVG/SMIL loops — so scrolling only moves GPU-composited layers (`transform`/`opacity`). No count-up numbers, no blanket fade-up, no floating cards. UI feedback is fast (≈150 ms, ease-out). `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` removes parallax (scenes still cross-fade).

**Performance rules for the voyage:** each scene is ≤ 3 full-screen SVG layers + small "actor" SVGs (truck, caravan, sun, moon); only scenes within ±1 of the current one stay visible; JS never edits SVG content on scroll, only `style.transform`/`style.opacity`; text halo and nav colour change per stage, not per frame.

## Signature: the voyage (full-page scroll background)

`landing/assets/img/world.html`, generated by `brand/build_world.py` (seeded, no dependencies — re-run after any change). It sits fixed behind the whole landing page; `qafila.js` maps the scroll position to a journey value `s` (0 → 4) using each section's `data-scene`:

| s | Scene | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Golfe de Tunis — Boukornine, Sidi Bou Saïd (white houses, blue doors and grilles, bougainvillea, minaret, blue balustrade), port of Radès (gantry cranes, containers), container ship | hero |
| 1 | Sahel — olive groves in rows, ribat, dashed-line road with the Qafila truck (drives with the scroll), « صفاقس / Sfax 127 » sign, prickly pears | services, suivi |
| 2 | Djérid — Tozeur carved-brick façades (diamond/chevron/cross motifs), palm grove, dunes | réseau, plateforme |
| 3 | Sahara at dusk — layered dunes, camel caravan with its cameleer | équipe |
| 4 | Sahara at night — Bedouin tent, fire, resting camels, stars, crescent moon | devis, footer |

Sky colours, sun path, moon/stars, clouds and the text halo (`--halo`) are interpolated from `s`; text turns Chaux after `s > 3.55`. Layers move with depth-based parallax; the next scene fades in over the current one. A left-side route rail (Tunis → Sahel → Djérid → Sahara → Arrivée) shows progress on wide screens.

## The « bon de route » label

A shipping label (Ink or Sable header strip, white paper, tabular figures, dashed tear line). Used for the tracking module and the quote form. Shapes are label-like: 6 px radius for controls and labels, 10 px for sheets — no pill buttons.

## Landing page structure

Centred, few words, one idea per screen: Hero (claim) → Services (4 tariffs on one sheet) → Suivi (label with the 4 stages) → Réseau (map + timetable) → Plateforme (HA diagram) → Équipe (portraits + the five demo clients, with the academic disclaimer) → Devis (label form) → footer under the night sky. Dense content sits on Chaux « sheets »; headings sit directly on the sky with a halo.
